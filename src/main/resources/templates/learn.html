About the Crime Investigation and Reporting System

The Crime Investigation and Reporting System is designed to provide a secure, fast, and transparent way for citizens to report crimes directly to law enforcement authorities. With this system, every voice matters, and together we can make our community safer.

Why Should You Use This System?

  • Quickly report crimes without visiting a police station.
  • Ensure transparency and accountability in law enforcement.
  • Track the status of your submitted reports online.
  • Contribute to community safety and crime prevention.

How Does It Help?

This system bridges the gap between citizens and police by enabling instant reporting, improving investigation timelines, and reducing paperwork delays. Authorities can prioritize emergencies while citizens remain informed about case updates.

Types of Crimes You Can Report

  • Theft, robbery, and burglary
  • Cybercrime and online fraud
  • Domestic violence and harassment
  • Missing persons
  • Drug-related activities
  • Traffic accidents and violations
  • Any other suspicious activity

Purpose of Citizen Involvement

Citizens play a vital role in crime prevention. Reporting crimes promptly allows police to act faster, ensures justice for victims, and strengthens trust between the community and law enforcement.

Rulebook (As per Bangladesh Guidelines)

  • All reports must be truthful; false reporting is punishable under the law.
  • Reports will be handled according to the Bangladesh Penal Code and Cyber Security Act.
  • Personal information of the complainant will be kept confidential unless required by law.
  • Harassment or misuse of this system is strictly prohibited.
  • Reports may be forwarded to appropriate authorities for investigation.
  • Citizens should cooperate with investigating officers for faster case resolution.
